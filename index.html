Old style, done right

Jonats fries its chicken the old way at a stand inside the San Francisco–Halang Agrivet compound, Biñan — crispy outside, juicy inside, sold by the box so nobody at the table has to share one piece. The kitchen goes further: silog plates with java rice, mojos, spaghetti and 12-inch pizzas, with party trays for 8–10. Three branches around Laguna — Halang Biñan, Laguna Belair, Milagrosa Calamba.
